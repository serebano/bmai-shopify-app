ACCESS / REQUIREMENT 4.5.4
No separate Busymate AI account, password, SSO, or two-factor login is required. Install on your Shopify development store, then open Apps > Busymate AI. Shopify authenticates the embedded app. No developer-owned Shopify admin credentials are needed.

1. Install and open the app. Home shows the assistant setup checklist and training counts for your store's products, policies and pages. If retrying an earlier failed installation, click Retry setup. Confirm Assistant provisioned is Done/Live.

2. Click Turn on the storefront assistant. The theme editor opens App embeds with Busymate AI assistant enabled. Click Save if there are unsaved changes. You can also open Online Store > Themes > Customize > App embeds and enable it manually.

3. Open Ask us in both the Theme Editor preview and the real storefront. Ask about a product or policy that exists in your test store. The answer should use that store's content. On a password-protected store, Home may say it cannot confirm the embed; verify it in the preview/storefront itself.

4. Billing > Choose a plan opens Shopify's hosted pricing page. Select Free and approve the $0 plan. Return to the app and check the selected plan; use Refresh status if necessary.

5. On a development store, Shopify labels paid tiers Free to test. Test a change to Growth, then Scale, then back to Free. Confirm the app returns correctly and Home still shows the assistant Live. Do not activate paid testing on a live merchant store.

6. Assistant settings: change the assistant name, Save, then start a new storefront chat to see the new name. Store connection > Re-train on my store updates the knowledge after catalog/policy changes.

7. Guest shoppers can ask product/policy questions without login. For order tests, create a test customer and order in your development store and use that store's native Shopify customer sign-in. No external Busymate credentials are required. The assistant must not disclose another customer's orders and must ask for confirmation before an order-changing action.

8. Ask for a person to test handoff. Uninstall from the development store, reinstall, and verify that Home opens and the assistant is restored.
